about zakhrafah

Where geometry becomes ornament

Zakhrafah began with a simple question: what if the geometry behind ornamental pattern-making — the grids, the symmetry, the repetition — could be handed directly to anyone who wanted to create with it? The studio turns that geometry into a canvas: choose a grid, build a pattern, and fill it in. No drafting table required.

Geometry first

Every pattern starts from a real grid system, drawn with the same underlying logic used in traditional ornamental design.

Made for anyone

No design background required. If you can click a shape, you can build a pattern facet by facet.

Always growing

New grids, motifs, and palettes are added to the library as the studio grows.

what you can do

The pattern, ready to fill

Zakhrafah turns hand-drafted geometric artwork into a vector canvas — every line stays crisp, and every facet is its own shape waiting for colour.

Pattern library

Choose from seven grid systems — Flower of Life, Square, Hexagon, Triangle, Diamond, Brick, and Isometric — each one a different starting geometry for your pattern.

Vector canvas

Every facet is its own fillable shape, so the linework stays sharp no matter how far you zoom in.

Custom palettes

Mix and save your own colour sets, or start from palettes drawn from gypsum, wood, and tile.

Symmetry-true fills

Colour one repeat of a pattern and it mirrors across the whole composition automatically.

Save & resume

Your progress stays in the browser, so you can leave a piece half-finished and come back to it later.

Export

Download the finished piece as SVG or PNG, ready to print, frame, or share.

how to use it

Four steps to a finished pattern

No drawing skill required — the geometry is already drafted. You bring the colour.

01

Choose a pattern

Pick a grid — Flower of Life, Square, Hexagon, Triangle, Diamond, Brick, or Isometric — to set the geometry you'll build on.

02

Pick a palette

Start from a preset palette or mix your own colours to match the piece you have in mind.

03

Fill each facet

Click a shape to fill it. If the pattern is symmetric, its mirrored facets fill along with it.

04

Export or save

Download your finished piece, or leave it as it is — it will be waiting when you return.

the creator

Isa Al Mannai

I'm Isa Al Mannai, a Bahraini visual artist, researcher, and designer passionate about Islamic geometric art, cultural heritage, and digital innovation. My work explores the intersection of traditional ornamentation and contemporary technology, creating new ways to preserve, reinterpret, and share Islamic art. Through ZAKHRAFAH Studio, I aim to make geometric pattern design more accessible, inspiring creativity while celebrating the richness of our artistic heritage.
